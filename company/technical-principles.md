# Company Technical Principles

- Evidence before assertion.
- Authority is question-specific.
- Governing architecture and deployed conformance are distinct.
- IaC intent does not prove deployed application conformance.
- Missing evidence must not be converted into certainty.
- Conceptual decomposition does not mandate deployment decomposition.
- Keep Harvey George and Serpfix product boundaries explicit.
- Share standards only where they genuinely reduce risk or burden.
- Preserve historical evidence, clearly labelled.
- Classify documentation by authority/status rather than assuming it is current truth.
- Prefer evolutionary, reversible change over unnecessary rewrites.
