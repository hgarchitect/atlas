# Serpfix — Overview

Serpfix is a distinct product architecture from Harvey George.

Its current governing interpretation has a thin shared-platform boundary owning portal/front door, identity/trust, control plane, tenant/product access, routing, cross-product navigation/handoff, and approval of work entering specialist engines.

SEO Intelligence is a specialist product-engine boundary behind that shared platform. Feed Intelligence is a separate product/capability stream with its own intended architecture/runtime boundary where currently known.
