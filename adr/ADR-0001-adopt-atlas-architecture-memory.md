# ADR-0001: Adopt Atlas architecture memory

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-10

## Decision
Use `hgarchitect/atlas` as Atlas's canonical durable operational and architectural memory for Harvey George Ltd.

Retain curated architectural meaning, decisions, evidence classes, source maps, debt and verification focus here. Do not duplicate implementation/runtime truth from owning repositories, AWS, Jira, Confluence, telemetry or working Drive documents.

Harvey George and Serpfix remain distinct product architectures. Company standards may be shared only where independently justified.

## Consequences
Temporary onboarding Drive handoffs become transitional. Durable conclusions should be promoted here. Fresh runtime/implementation questions still require owning-source verification.
